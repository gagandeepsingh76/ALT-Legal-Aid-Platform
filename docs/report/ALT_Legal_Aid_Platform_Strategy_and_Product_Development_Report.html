1. Executive Summary

Project ALT (Access to Legal Aid through Technology) operates as a targeted legal aid initiative assisting undertrial prisoners across three prison complexes in Delhi: Tihar, Rohini, and Mandoli. The platform explicitly highlights a stated North Star mission metric — Days of Incarceration Saved — with an organizational aspiration of tracking toward 1,000,000 days of pretrial incarceration prevented.

A functional, AI-enabled software platform has already been built and deployed to support this mission. The platform incorporates end-to-end case tracking, document collection checklists, AI-assisted legal charge decoding (BNS/IPC), automated bail strategy drafting, and volunteer lawyer matching. However, the platform is not yet in real use by the ground team. The small team — comprising legal associates, field associates, and social workers — splits its time between field visits and desk operations, continuing to manage casework, client interactions, and court tracking across spreadsheets, telephone logs, and physical notebooks.

This report provides a formal 1-Month Product Adoption and Pilot Strategy. The strategic approach is grounded in an operational principle: The platform must not force the ground team to alter their core defense work; rather, the software must be incrementally embedded into existing field and desk routines while providing immediate, tangible value to the legal team. By establishing structured desk rituals, activating AI bail drafting tools early, and implementing a 3-stage safe deprecation of spreadsheets, Project ALT can transition the platform into the primary operational system of record while substantially mitigating operational risks to active legal casework. To demonstrate how these recommendations can be concretely operationalized, an interactive product prototype has been independently developed and publicly deployed as detailed in Section 12.

2. Understanding the Current ALT Platform

[Verified Fact] An empirical evaluation of the live platform at https://altindia.base44.app/demo reveals a functional system organized around six global case stages and four primary modules:

1. Executive Dashboard (/demo): Provides macro-level tracking of active caseloads (2,773 cases), stage distribution (Documentation: 1,401; Case Decoding: 1,051; Bail Strategy: 323), resolution rates (66% bail granted), and partner legal capacity (169 available out of 241 registered advocates).

2. Case Inventory and Intake (/demo/cases): A centralized repository supporting both List and Kanban Board views. It incorporates multi-criteria filtering by trial stage, call status, and Category B statutory eligibility (identifying prisoners eligible under Section 436A CrPC / Section 479 BNSS). A structured + New Case Intake modal captures prisoner demographics, facility details, arrest dates, family contact points, and applicable legal sections.

3. Case File 360° Hub: An individual case workbench structured into eight functional tabs: (i) Overview (demographics, timeline, incarceration impact, socioeconomic debt status); (ii) Documentation (9-item verification checklist including FIR, remand orders, and charge sheets); (iii) Case Decoding (AI analysis of offenses, allegations, and bail eligibility); (iv) Bail Strategy (bail tracker, hearing calendar, AI-synthesized grounds, and legal precedents); (v) Monitoring (court appearance logs and family check-in records); (vi) Tasks; (vii) Notes; and (viii) Calendar.

4. Social Legal Partner Network & Matching Engine (/demo/volunteers, /demo/matching): A roster of 241 legal partners cross-referenced against court jurisdictions (Saket, Tis Hazari, Rohini, Karkardooma, Patiala House), offense specializations, and active caseload ratios (e.g., 2/15 cases). The AI Matching Engine algorithmically evaluates and ranks candidates based on court presence and bandwidth.

3. The Product Adoption Problem

The fundamental challenge at Project ALT is not software reliability or technical functionality. It is an Operational Integration Disconnect between desktop-centric software design and the fast-paced, split-environment workflow of legal aid casework.

Operational Dimension Field & Ground Reality (How Team Works) Current Platform Architecture (What Is Built)
Data Capture Context Prison visits and mulakats on one side; calling families, taking updates, and tracking progress from the office on the other. Structured desktop web interface with multi-tab data entry forms and 9-point document checklists.
Caseload Integrity Risk Team relies on established spreadsheets because missing a court hearing carries severe consequences for clients. Platform exists in parallel without a formal migration protocol, creating fear of dual entry and lost data.
Perceived Value Staff perceive platform data entry as administrative reporting rather than an immediate aid to daily work. High-value automated features (AI decoding, bail grounds) require initial data entry before showing utility.

4. Why Adoption Is Currently Low

A diagnostic evaluation of ground operations identifies four systemic factors contributing to low adoption:

1. Split Workflow Between Field and Desk [Verified Fact / Operational Ingestion]: As established in the project context, the team's work is split between field visits (prison visits and mulakats) and office desk work (calling families, taking updates, tracking progress in spreadsheets). Field visits generate quick, unformatted notes. When staff return to the office, transcribing these notes across multiple web tabs introduces extra friction compared to updating an existing spreadsheet.

2. The Dual-Entry Burden [Strategic Recommendation]: In the absence of an operational transition protocol, entering data into ALT represents redundant effort on top of active spreadsheets. Under daily workload pressures, staff default to the familiar spreadsheets they already maintain.

3. High Latency to Value Realization: To benefit from AI Case Decoding or automated bail grounds, users must first complete demographic intake and upload case paperwork. This delay causes users to perceive ALT as administrative overhead rather than a tool that saves them time.

4. Cognitive Overload from Monolithic Feature Exposure: Presenting all six stages, eight tabs, partner rosters, and matching systems simultaneously creates operational intimidation for a small team managing urgent casework.

5. Proposed Adoption Strategy

[Strategic Recommendation] To overcome operational inertia, we introduce a structured Adoption Bridge governed by the principle: "Integrate ALT into the team's existing physical and desk habits rather than attempting an abrupt digital overhaul."

  • Preserve Natural Field Habits: Allow associates to maintain quick notebook jottings during prison visits; introduce ALT strictly as a structured 10-minute desk ritual upon office return.
  • Accelerate Value Realization: Expose AI Case Decoding and automated bail petition grounds early to legal associates to provide projected drafting time savings per case.
  • Sequential Module Rollout: Introduce Intake & Overview (Week 1) → Documentation & Monitoring (Week 2) → AI Decoding & Bail Strategy (Week 3) → Matching & Cutover (Week 4).
  • Safe, 3-Stage Spreadsheet Sunset: Transition spreadsheets from Active Parallel Tracker → Controlled Split → Locked Read-Only Archive to substantially mitigate operational disruption to active cases.

6. Role-Based Workflow Integration

A. Field Associate (Prison Visits to Structured Intake):
Workflow: Prison Mulakat (Field Notes) → Office Return → 10-Minute Post-Mulakat Sync → Live in ALT.
Field associates continue conducting in-person interviews at prison visitor rooms using notebooks. Upon returning to the office, they open + New Case Intake and spend 5–8 minutes recording prisoner demographics, facility details, arrest dates, and family contact numbers. This eliminates repetitive queries from colleagues seeking prisoner details.

B. Social Worker (Document Collection & Family Outreach):
Workflow: Family Calling → Collect Court Records → Toggle 9-Item Checklist → "Docs Ready" Tag.
Social workers filter the Cases list by Stage: Documentation. They conduct family outreach calls, record socioeconomic debt context in the Monitoring Tab, obtain copy documents from court registries, and toggle checklist items (Requested → Received → Verified). The visual progress bar replaces cluttered spreadsheet columns.

C. Legal Associate (AI Decoding, Bail Drafting & Court Sync):
Workflow: Filter "Docs Ready" → Review AI Decoding → Copy AI Bail Grounds → File & Log Hearing.
Legal associates filter for cases marked Docs Ready. They review AI-extracted BNS/IPC offenses and statutory bail eligibility in the Case Decoding Tab, utilize synthesized grounds and citations from the Bail Strategy Tab to assist in petition drafting, attend court hearings, and log results in the in-app calendar.

8. Spreadsheet Transition Strategy

[Strategic Recommendation] To ensure that ongoing court proceedings and statutory bail deadlines are protected, spreadsheets are retired across three controlled operational phases:

Figure 4: Three-Stage Spreadsheet Sunsetting Model [Strategic Recommendation]

Stage 1: Parallel Run (Weeks 1–2): Spreadsheets remain accessible as a secondary reference. Field staff log data directly into ALT; the Product Lead verifies consistency in legacy sheets, avoiding duplicate entry burdens on field associates.
Stage 2: Controlled Split (Week 3): Documentation checklists and bail tracking columns are locked in spreadsheets. Because AI drafting features exist solely on ALT, active tracking naturally shifts to the platform.
Stage 3: Full Operational Cutover (Week 4 Onward): Spreadsheets are marked [ARCHIVED - READ ONLY] and locked. All legal aid workflows operate within ALT, supported by automated weekly CSV database backups for administrative security.

9. Adoption Rituals and Feedback Loop

[Strategic Recommendation] To cultivate repeatable operational habits without adding meeting fatigue, three micro-rituals totaling less than 20 minutes daily are instituted:

1. Morning 5-Minute Kanban Standup (09:30 AM • 5 mins): The entire ground team reviews the Cases Board View to identify "Docs Ready" cases, review scheduled hearings for the day, and clear pipeline bottlenecks.
2. Post-Mulakat Desk Sync (02:30 PM • 10 mins): Upon returning from field visits, field associates spend 5–10 minutes entering notebook jottings into structured ALT prisoner profiles.
3. Friday Pipeline Retrospective (Fridays 04:30 PM • 15 mins): Legal associates and coordinators review granted bails, log Incarceration Days Saved, confirm next week's court dates, and evaluate friction logs.

Rapid Feedback Channel: A dedicated internal communication thread (#alt-pilot-feedback) captures 30-second friction reports from staff (e.g., missing legal sections in dropdowns). The Lead Legal Associate serves as the single decision owner to resolve operational bottlenecks rapidly.

12. Product Prototype and Implementation Demonstration

[Implementation Demonstration] To operationalize and visualize the strategic recommendations outlined in Sections 5 through 9, an independent product prototype has been engineered and publicly deployed. This prototype demonstrates how the proposed workflows — including daily action prioritization, structured docket inspection, non-disruptive spreadsheet migration, and advocate-controlled AI synthesis — can be translated into an intuitive, high-usability legal operations platform for field teams and advocates.

12.1 Purpose of the Prototype

The prototype was developed to demonstrate how the strategic recommendations in this report could be translated into a practical advocate-facing legal operations interface. While the existing Project ALT system provides extensive technical capability, the prototype specifically explores how daily legal workflows can be made easier to understand, navigate, and adopt during high-pressure court and field operations.

12.2 Strategy-to-Product Mapping

The interactive prototype directly embodies the strategic interventions proposed in this report:

Strategic Recommendation Prototype Implementation Intended Operational Benefit
1. Centralized Operational Visibility Advocate dashboard featuring today's priorities, active dockets, hearing status, pending documents, and client follow-up indicators. Reduces fragmented tracking across multiple disparate tools and paper notes.
2. Structured Case Workflow Unified case file workspace with 6-stage lifecycle stepper, case details, notes timeline, document checklist, tasks, and audit logs. Provides a clearer single operational view of each case for legal and field staff.
3. Hearing and Deadline Visibility Dedicated Hearings interface with courtroom briefs, judge bench information, and "Hearing Today" priority alert badges. Helps advocates rapidly identify and prepare for urgent court actions.

6. AI-Assisted Legal Operations AI Case Insights split-view interface pairing source records (FIR, remand logs) with structured synthesis (Summary, Facts, Dates, Grounds). Demonstrates how AI-supported case synthesis can fit seamlessly into the broader legal workflow under advocate review.
7. Accessibility and Usability Full English/Hindi bilingual switching, Light Mode, Dark Mode, and an independent Eye Comfort Mode. Supports diverse linguistic preferences and reduces ocular strain during extended legal reading.

13. Conclusion

The ALT Legal Aid Platform represents a purpose-built technological system designed to assist undertrial legal defense in India. The lack of adoption observed to date is an operational gap between desktop software interfaces and the split field-and-desk working reality of legal aid teams.

By acknowledging the practicalities of field visits, instituting a 10-minute post-visit desk routine, delivering drafting utility to legal associates through AI Case Decoding, and safely sunsetting spreadsheets across three distinct phases, this strategy establishes an operational bridge to sustainable platform adoption.

Through this 1-month pilot, Project ALT can transition its ground operations to the platform — accelerating bail filings, eliminating administrative fragmentation, and advancing toward its stated mission of preventing unjust pretrial incarceration. The accompanying product prototype confirms that these workflow principles are not merely conceptual, but immediately actionable in software.

Executive Strategy Synthesis
1. Operational Grounding:
Accommodates physical field note-taking; embeds ALT as a lightweight desk routine.		 2. Immediate Advocacy Value:
Activates AI Case Decoding and bail grounds early to provide drafting efficiency and win staff buy-in.		 3. Safe Transition:
Phases out spreadsheets in 3 controlled stages, mitigating operational disruption to active cases.
